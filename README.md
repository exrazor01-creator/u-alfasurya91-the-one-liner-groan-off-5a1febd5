## The entry

> Dad said he'd teach me to grill. Step one: stare at the coals until they feel judged.

85 characters, one line, plain ASCII — inside the 1–140 character gate.

## Why this line

Written against the judge's brief, in the order it weighs things:

1. **A real dad joke** — a pun/twist a dad would tell out loud, built on the dad-at-the-grill stereotype.
2. **Punchline lands fast** — the turn is the word "judged": the coals are not just stared at, they are judged, like contestants before a stern critic.
3. **Fresh** — steers clear of the worn-out "Hi hungry, I'm Dad" crack and every other recycled dinner-table pun.
4. **Tight and rhythmic** — two short sentences in one line, easy to say out loud and easy to pass on.
5. **Groan earned** — silly enough to make people laugh and sharp enough to be worth repeating.
